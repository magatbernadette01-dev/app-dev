# My Favorite Series: Alice in Borderland

*Genre:* Action, Thriller, Sci-Fi  
*Streaming on:* [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/title/80200575)

Alice in Borderland is a Japanese series about *Arisu*, a gamer who ends up in an empty, alternate Tokyo where he must play deadly games to survive. Each challenge tests his *intelligence*, *courage*, and *teamwork*.  

## Why I Like It
- It’s full of suspense and creative games.  
- The story has unexpected twists.  
- It makes me think about survival and the value of life.  

*Overall*, Alice in Borderland is a thrilling and thought-provoking show that keeps you on edge from start to finish.
